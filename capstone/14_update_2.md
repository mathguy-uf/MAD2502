# Update 2: Show Real Progress

[Capstone home](README.md) · [First Python Result](03_first_python_result.md)

**Update 2 is a 10-point evidence checkpoint. Submit one PDF per group.** Follow Canvas for the exact grading breakdown and submission instructions.

The goal is to show that your project is real and moving forward. A small reproducible result is useful evidence; a list of future plans alone is not.

Include:

1. **Your current research question and any change since Update 1.** State the specific question you are now investigating. If the question, source, scope, or method changed, describe what changed and why, using actual evidence from your work. If the question stayed the same, say how your work progressed.
2. **Your actual source and data.** Identify where the information came from and what a row or record represents. For tabular data, show the first five rows, last five rows, and dimensions using `head()`, `tail()`, and `shape`.
3. **Python code and actual output.** Show a meaningful piece of code your group has run and the result it produced.
4. **One first result.** Include a figure, table, count, summary, or other concrete result, with a sentence explaining what it shows.
5. **A problem or failed attempt.** Document an obstacle or branch of your research tree, the evidence you observed, what you learned, and what you changed or will try next.
6. **Individual contributions and next tasks.** State what each member has done and what each will do next.

You do not need to force a regression, classification model, simulation, or optimization method into this checkpoint. Use a method appropriate to the question and your current progress. A changed direction or unsuccessful attempt is acceptable when you show genuine work and explain the evidence.

Keep notes using **question → action → evidence → next step**. Those notes will help you write the [final report](08_final_report.md).
